---
name: enforce-log-output-standards
description: Use this skill when generating log outputs to ensure they meet specified formatting and content rules.
---
1. Verify that all service names in the output are in lower-case with '-' replaced by '_'.
2. Ensure that timestamps are formatted in UTC and follow the specified format (YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ).
3. Confirm that the output JSON structure adheres to the required schema, including necessary fields and their types.
